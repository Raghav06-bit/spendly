Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we handle your data.

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects only the data necessary to provide our expense tracking service. This includes:

  • Account information: Email address and password (securely hashed) when you create an account.
  • Expense data: Transaction details you enter — amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and notes.
  • Usage data: Basic interaction data to improve the service (e.g., which features are used).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to provide and improve the Spendly service:

  • To authenticate you and secure your account.
  • To store, display, and organize your expense records.
  • To generate summaries, charts, and insights within your dashboard.
  • To communicate important service updates or security notices.

We do not use your financial data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond providing the service to you.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely and never leaves our infrastructure:

  • All data is stored in a local database on our servers.
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt).
  • Database connections use secure, authenticated channels.
  • We implement access controls so only you can access your data.

You retain full ownership of your expense data. You can export or delete it at any time from your account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The only external requests made are:

  • Google Fonts: For loading the DM Sans and DM Serif Display typefaces (loaded from fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com).

No personal or financial data is sent to any third party. If we ever add integrations (e.g., bank sync), we will request your explicit consent and update this policy.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all data we hold about you in your dashboard.
  • Export: Download your expense data in a portable format.
  • Deletion: Permanently delete your account and all associated data.
  • Correction: Update or correct any inaccurate expense entries.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

privacy@spendly.example.com
